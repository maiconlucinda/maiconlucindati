About

I'm a Cloud Support Engineer, Security at AWS, helping enterprise customers investigate, troubleshoot, and secure their cloud environments. I work daily with Amazon GuardDuty, AWS Security Hub, AWS Config, Amazon Inspector, AWS CloudTrail, Amazon Macie, IAM Identity Center, and AWS Organizations, analyzing security logs and telemetry to identify risks, misconfigurations, and vulnerabilities before they become incidents.

Before AWS, I worked as a Security and Network Engineer at eCom Solutions, managing SOC operations, firewall administration, and incident triage across enterprise environments. Originally from Bahia, Brazil, I moved to Dublin, Ireland to pursue my Master's degree, on top of a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering. I hold certifications across AWS, Check Point, Palo Alto Networks, F5, and Cisco, and I keep sharpening my offensive and defensive skills on TryHackMe.
